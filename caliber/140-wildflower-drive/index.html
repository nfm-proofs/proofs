Some of High Hampton's most cherished homes are the Sheep Laurel Cottages, admired for their approachable scale, classic architecture, and easy connection to the Club. At 140 Wildflower Drive, three private bedroom suites sit within a connected series of indoor and outdoor gathering spaces.

From the Tom Fazio Course to Dinner at the Inn

Set at 3,500 feet and spanning 1,400 acres, High Hampton brings together an 18-hole course reimagined by Tom Fazio, a multi-court racquet club, tennis, pickleball and croquet, fly-fishing, a historic dahlia garden, miles of paths and trails, and a lakeside area with docks and a pool. Dining ranges from Halsted House and The Range House to Mitch's Lakeside.

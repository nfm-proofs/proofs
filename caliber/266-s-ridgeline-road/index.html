Lake Life, Custom-Built for the Way You Live

Five Bedrooms, Designed for Gathering on Lake Glenville

Interiors by Kristen Wadsworth Designs

Well underway in Tocami with interiors designed by Kristen Wadsworth Designs, this 5 bedroom, 5 bathroom, and 2 half-bath home marries timeless architecture, exceptional craftsmanship, and a layout made for the way people gather to enjoy life on Lake Glenville.

Lake and Mountain Views, Thoughtfully Captured

Positioned to capture lake and mountain views, every space has been thoughtfully planned, from the welcoming front porch to the expansive outdoor living areas inviting you to take in the surroundings.

Where Indoors and Out Become One

The main level has a vaulted great room where an accordion glass door opens the entire back of the home to a covered deck with a stone fireplace and grilling kitchen, the transition between indoors and out feels effortless.

A Private Wing, A Peaceful Retreat

On a private wing, the primary suite includes a screened porch overlooking the lake, creating a peaceful retreat just steps from the main living spaces.

Upstairs, Privacy for Visitors

A private guest suite upstairs offers separation for visitors.

The Lower Level Expands the Gathering

The lower level expands the home's gathering spaces with a family room, fireplace, covered deck, an additional guest suite, and two more bedrooms connected by a shared bath.

Room to Grow, Whenever You're Ready

While an unfinished bonus room above the garage offers flexibility for future use.

Natural Stone, Warm Wood, Timeless Details

Throughout the home, natural stone, warm wood accents, custom cabinetry, shiplap detailing, designer lighting, and premium appliances create timeless interiors.

Custom Quality, Mountain Setting

Every finish has been selected to complement the home's mountain setting while offering the quality expected of a custom build. Life at Tocami extends well beyond your front door.

Life at Tocami: Built Around the Lake

Designed around the lake and love for the outdoors, this intimate lakefront community offers recreation and relaxation.

A Deeded Boat Slip for Every Homesite

Every homesite includes its own deeded boat slip, while community amenities include pickleball, walking trails, hatchet throwing, and a vintage barn that is the social heart of the community.

The Lakeside Clubhouse & GlenCove Membership

Plus, the lakeside clubhouse at the docks is underway and promises even more opportunities to enjoy life on the lake. Ownership at Tocami also includes a PREPAID membership to GlenCove by Old Edwards (annual dues not included), providing access to a wide array of amenities, including a 12-hole par-3 golf course, wellness center, ponds, community gardens, resort-style pool with splash pad, and the Entertainment Barn with dining and more.

Tucked Away, Yet Close to Everything

Just minutes to the Cashiers Crossroads, life at Tocami feels tucked away yet close to everything.

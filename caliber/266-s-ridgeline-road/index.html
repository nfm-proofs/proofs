Life at Tocami: Built Around the Lake

Life at Tocami extends well beyond your front door.

Designed around the lake and love for the outdoors, this intimate lakefront community offers recreation and relaxation.

Every homesite includes its own deeded boat slip, while community amenities include pickleball, walking trails, hatchet throwing, and a barn that is the social heart of the community.

Tocami, forty wooded acres on the shoreline of Lake Glenville, high on the Highlands-Cashiers Plateau, with the pinpoint marking the 266 S Ridgeline Road homesite.

A deeded boat slip with every homesite, and long summer days spent on the water.

A canvas tent in the trees, a stone fire pit, and the barn at the community's heart.

Plus, the lakeside clubhouse at the docks is underway and promises even more opportunities to enjoy life on the lake.

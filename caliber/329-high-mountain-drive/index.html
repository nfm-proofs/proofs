The Club, a Short Walk or Cart Ride Away

Mountaintop Golf & Lake Club is the premier equity membership resort-style community on the Highlands-Cashiers Plateau. The opportunity for membership is by invitation only. This world-famous Club focuses on providing an exceptional Western North Carolina mountain experience through its outstanding Tom Fazio-designed golf course while exuding a friendly ambiance and genteel charm. Mountaintop Members enjoy rustic and refined adventures, exquisite cuisine, and luxurious Lake Glenville recreation.

Amenities at Mountaintop Golf & Lake Club, including Lake Glenville recreation, the sports courts, and the clubhouse. These photographs depict Club amenities in the surrounding community and are not part of the property.

Mountaintop Golf & Lake Club is the ideal destination for unforgettable outdoor fun amidst the beautiful Southern Appalachian Mountains. With Mountaintop's amenities only a short walk or golf cart ride away, this property is an effortless way to enjoy the best of Club life while having a retreat all its own.

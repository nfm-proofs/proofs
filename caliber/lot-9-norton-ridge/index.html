Lot 9 in Norton Ridge

Lot 9 in Norton Ridge offers a gently sloping homesite nestled among beautiful hardwood trees, with construction drawings included for a proposed 5,180-square-foot mountain home.

The homesite in fall color.

Rustic Mountain Character, Timeless Craftsman Detail

The architectural design blends rustic mountain character with timeless craftsman details, featuring natural stone, shingle siding, dramatic gables, and generous covered outdoor living spaces.

Rendering of the proposed rear elevation and covered outdoor living. Home not yet built.

A Plan Made for Hosting Family and Friends

The proposed floor plan includes four bedrooms, plus a fifth bedroom suite over the garage, an open-concept layout, a main-level primary suite, and a walk-out lower level ideal for hosting family and friends.

Proposed main-level floor plan, included with the construction drawings.

Proposed walk-out lower-level floor plan.

A Clear Place to Start

A topographic survey is also available, and the property offers access to community water and sewer systems, subject to applicable requirements and connection fees. This is an incredible value for someone who wants to build a new house but doesn’t know where to start.

The Lot 9 marker at the homesite.

A Gated Community Between Highlands and Cashiers

Norton Ridge by Old Edwards is a gated community of multimillion-dollar homes, conveniently located off Norton Road between Highlands and Cashiers.

The Norton Ridge community pavilion, a shared amenity, not part of Lot 9.

A Short Walk or Golf Cart Ride to GlenCove

Just a short walk or golf cart ride from GlenCove by Old Edwards, the property offers easy access to recreational and wellness amenities, including a 12-hole par-3 golf course, swimming pool, fitness center and spa, hiking trails, lake activities, pickleball, and the Entertainment Barn. GlenCove membership is optional and is not required to purchase the property.
